Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Global Skills & Knowledge Academy Limited handles information that may be provided when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who We Are

Global Skills & Knowledge Academy Limited is an organisation based in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria, providing technology solutions, professional training, education-related services and research and knowledge-development activities.

For questions about this Privacy Notice or how information is handled, please contact us through the details on our Contact page.

2. Information We May Receive

The information we receive depends on how you interact with our website and services. It may include:

  • Contact information such as your name and telephone number when you voluntarily provide them in an enquiry.
  • Details about your enquiry, training interests, project requirements or institutional needs.
  • Information contained in messages or documents you choose to send to us.
  • Basic technical information that may be recorded by website hosting or security infrastructure, such as browser information, access times and request data.

We do not ask visitors to provide personal information merely to browse the public pages of this website.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily shared with us may be used for legitimate operational and communication purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Discussing training programmes, technology services and education solutions.
  • Understanding project requirements and responding to institutional or business enquiries.
  • Maintaining website functionality, reliability and security.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. WhatsApp and External Services

Our website provides links to WhatsApp for communication. When you follow these links and communicate with us, your interaction is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

Our website may also link to external services such as Google Maps. These third-party services operate independently and have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their respective policies before using them.

5. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential technical functionality, including browser-based settings such as language preferences. Website hosting infrastructure may also use technical mechanisms necessary for service delivery and security.

We do not claim to operate advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies on this website. If additional analytics or tracking technologies are introduced, this notice should be reviewed and updated accordingly.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information provided through our website. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers involved in website hosting, communication, security or service delivery, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Information Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, including responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

8. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our operations. However, no internet-based communication or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through ordinary website enquiries or messaging channels.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information relating to you, including requesting access, correction or deletion, and raising concerns about how information is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details on our Contact page. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

10. Children's Information

Our educational activities may be relevant to learners of different ages. Where information relating to children is handled in connection with an educational service, appropriate care and applicable legal requirements should be observed. Parents, guardians and authorised institutions should contact us where clarification is needed.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our website, services or applicable legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Notice, please contact Global Skills & Knowledge Academy Limited.
